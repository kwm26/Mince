# Recipe Research

24 healthy, high-protein recipes for bodybuilding and meal prep (4 breakfasts, 20 mains), gathered by web research on established recipe, dietitian and fitness sites. Each recipe page was fetched and checked; nothing was invented.

- **Macros** are per serving, exactly as stated on the source page ("–" means not stated).
- **Ingredient lists** are recorded as facts. **Steps are paraphrased** in our own words, not copied. Always credit and link the source in the app.
- Shelf life, pack sizes and some quantity conversions in `data/*.json` are **estimates** (`estimated: true`, `qtyNote`).
- Selection favored recipes that **reuse the same perishables**, since Mince optimizes for that.

## Data gaps & doubts

- **1. Greek Chicken Protein Bowl with Garlic Yogurt:** Post states both 50 g and 51 g protein; Total time excludes optional 30-min marinade.
- **2. Chicken Fried Rice:** 45 min assumes rice already cooked and chilled.
- **3. One-Pan Roasted Chicken, Broccoli & Sweet Potatoes:** Stated times add to 40 min vs 37 listed; Chicken weight not given; Macros marked approximate.
- **4. Italian-Style Skillet Chicken with Tomatoes and Mushrooms:** Olive oil amount and chicken weight not stated; Macros auto-calculated.
- **5. Chicken and Zucchini Skillet:** No cooking oil listed (relies on nonstick pan).
- **6. Easiest Sheet Pan Turkey Zucchini Meatballs:** Only protein stated ('over 30 g in 4 meatballs'); Servings implied (4), not stated; Oil/salt/pepper amounts not listed.
- **7. Greek Turkey Meatballs with Tzatziki:** Servings blank on recipe card (4 is an assumption); Macros auto-calculated; serving size unclear; Total time excludes optional 1-hr chill.
- **8. Ginger Pork Stir Fry:** Listed 30 min excludes suggested 15–30 min marinade; Macros include rice.
- **9. Healthy Sweet and Sour Pork Tenderloin Stir-Fry:** Uses reduced-sugar ketchup and zero-cal sweetener (less whole-food); Macros include rice.
- **10. Pork Medallions with Mushroom Sauce:** Card doesn't say nutrition is per serving; Title says 15 min, card says 20.
- **11. Viral Sweet Potato Beef Bowls:** Servings not stated (4 inferred from 4 oz beef per bowl).
- **12. Steak Fajita Bowls:** Card says 45 min, intro says 30; Uses guacamole rather than whole avocado.
- **13. Greek Yogurt Parfait Bowl:** Source URL is the print page.
- **15. High-Protein Breakfast Burrito (Tofu Scramble):** Exactly at 25 g breakfast minimum.
- **16. Ham & Cottage Cheese Frittata with Spinach:** Exactly at 25 g breakfast minimum; Contains ham (processed meat); Excludes 5–10 min rest.
- **17. Shrimp and Broccoli Stir Fry:** Unclear whether nutrition includes rice.
- **18. Sheet Pan Salmon, Broccoli and Potatoes:** Uses garlic powder, not fresh garlic; Fillet weight not given.
- **19. Lemon Garlic Baked Cod:** Intro says under 30 min, card says 20; Ingredients list cilantro, steps say parsley.
- **20. Cilantro Lime Salmon Bake with Tomato Avocado Salsa:** Cilantro amount for sauce missing on page; High fat (48 g) from mayo sauce; Source URL is the print page.
- **21. High-Protein Tofu Buddha Bowl:** Macros estimated; Real time likely >45 min (sweet potatoes must cool).
- **22. Shrimp and Vegetables Skillet:** Uses packaged chili seasoning; Source URL is the print page.
- **23. Lentil and Mushroom Ragu:** Carbs and fat not stated; Relies on packaged red lentil spaghetti.
- **24. Spinach and Tomato Tuna Salad with Greek Yogurt:** Small family blog — consider Born Fitness backup.
